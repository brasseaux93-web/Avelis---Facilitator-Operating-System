### 1. Purpose

The Host intends to evaluate Avelis as the session environment for facilitated talks that **must not produce a transcript** — mediation, shuttle, ombuds, or settlement conversations the Host already runs.

Avelis is a facilitator operating system: a live room, a disclosed conflict agent, a closed-vocabulary process ledger, and a destruction receipt. It is not a recording product, not an AI judge, and not a consumer mediation app.

### 2. Evaluation period

**Ninety (90) days** from the date both sides have signed below, or until the Host ends the evaluation in writing, whichever is first.

No fee is due for the evaluation. The Host is not obligated to buy. Avelis is not obligated to keep a seat open after the period ends.

### 3. What Avelis will provide

1. One organization seat for named facilitators at the Host.
2. Invite-only party access. Parties do not create standing accounts.
3. A live room in which messages are delivered and dropped. Optional mesh voice, if used, is browser-to-browser; Avelis does not receive audio frames.
4. A disclosed conflict agent. If inference is configured, a RAM window of live text may be sent to Groq while the room is open. That path is shown to every party before they speak. The agent does not write the ledger.
5. A process ledger (opened, invited, tabled, agreed, closed — not quotation) and an optional joint minute the Host authors.
6. Retention chosen by the Host in draft (0–30 days), locked when a session opens, then enforced. After purge, a destruction receipt remains.
7. The evaluation DPA and subprocessor list, for counsel, before the first session.
8. Founder availability for the first three sessions — process support, not a substitute facilitator.

### 4. What the Host will do

1. Run **at least three (3) real sessions** on the Host’s own matter type. Demo scripts do not count.
2. After those sessions, send Avelis a short written note: what they would not run on a transcript machine, and what still failed.
3. Not request a transcript, captions, recording, settlement score, or party directory. If a matter requires a file of the talk, that matter stays off Avelis.

The Host remains the facilitator. Parties remain the Host’s parties. Avelis does not appear as counsel, mediator of record, or a party.

### 5. Speech and process

Room messages are delivered live and are not stored by Avelis. Audio is not recorded. Avelis cannot produce a transcript it never made.

This letter does **not** create attorney–client privilege, mediator privilege, or immunity from process. Privilege, if any, is the Host’s existing professional duty — not a feature of the software.

Process records and any joint minute are retained only until the deadline the Host set, then destroyed. The destruction receipt is hashes and timestamps, not speech.

### 6. Intent if the chamber holds

If, at the end of the period, the Host judges the chamber fit for its practice, the Host **intends** to enter a one-year organization-seat agreement at the then-current rate for the Host’s size:

| Seat | Indicative annual rate (USD) |
|---|---|
| Practice (1–3 facilitators) | $14,400 |
| Firm / ADR office | $54,000 |

Rates are indicative, not a quote. A signed order or MSA supersedes this letter. Conversion is optional.

### 7. What this letter is not

- Not a purchase order or invoice.
- Not exclusivity. The Host may keep using other rooms.
- Not a public case study without the Host’s written consent. Avelis may say that a design-partner evaluation occurred, without naming the Host, unless the Host agrees otherwise.
- Not permission to store speech, add party accounts, or turn the agent into an undisclosed listener.

### 8. Confidentiality

Each side will treat the other’s non-public evaluation notes and session process (ledger types, retention choices, destruction receipts) as confidential for two years, except as required by law. Session **talk** is not confidential *to Avelis* because Avelis does not keep it.

### 9. Signatures

By signing, each side confirms it has read the evaluation DPA and this letter, and that the person signing may bind that side to a **non-binding** statement of intent.
