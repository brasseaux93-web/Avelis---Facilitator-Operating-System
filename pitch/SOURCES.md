# Market sources

Accessed September 2026. Headline ADR/privacy figures are **context**, not Avelis revenue. Working TAM is constructed in [FINANCIAL_MODEL.md](FINANCIAL_MODEL.md).

## Alternative dispute resolution (professional services)

- 360iResearch, *Alternative Dispute Services Market* (2026–2032): market **USD 9.13 billion in 2025**, **USD 9.73 billion in 2026**, **USD 14.33 billion by 2032**, CAGR **6.64%**. Digital/ODR and virtual hearings noted as adoption drivers. https://www.360iresearch.com/library/intelligence/alternative-dispute-services
- Research and Markets (March 2025 release): Alternative Dispute Services **USD 8.47 billion (2024)** growing at **6.54% CAGR to USD 12.43 billion by 2030**.
- Research and Markets / MarketGlass, *Conflict Resolution Solutions*: **USD 10 billion (2024)** to **USD 13.8 billion by 2030**, CAGR **5.6%**. Mediation segment to **USD 4.1 billion by 2030** (7.1% CAGR). US market **USD 2.7 billion (2024)**.

## Case volume (providers)

- JAMS 2025 Resolution Report: **21,939 new cases**; **28,345 active**; **308 international new** (~**+16%** YoY). https://markets.financialcontent.com/wss/article/bizwire-2026-5-7-jams-reports-rising-global-demand-for-adr-in-2025-resolution-report
- JAMS company reporting: nearly **500 neutrals**, **29 locations**; largest private ADR provider.
- AAA Infographics FY2025: mediation filings represented **USD 2.4 billion** in disputes; median **112 days** to settle. https://feature.adr.org/2025-aaa-infographics
- AAA (19 Feb 2026): mediation caseload **+14% since 2022**; 2025 commercial filings **+15% YoY**; consumer filings **+79% YoY**.

## Practitioner demand

- ALM research commissioned by JAMS (*State of ADR*): nearly **90%** of surveyed US lawyers used mediation in the past year (n=108 senior practitioners plus interviews). Law.com, 30 Jan 2026.

## Adjacent software (not Avelis TAM)

- ComplexDiscovery eDiscovery mashup 2025–2030: worldwide **USD 19.61 billion (2025)** to **USD 28.08 billion (2030)**, CAGR **7.44%**. Cited as the cost of persistence — the industry that exists because conversations became records.
- MarketIntelo, Corporate Investigations Software: **USD 4.2 billion (2025)** to **USD 9.3 billion (2034)**, CAGR **9.8%**. Case-file software; Avelis is not this product.
- MarketIntelo, Secure Messaging: **USD 12.4 billion (2025)** to **USD 42.8 billion (2034)**, CAGR **14.2%**. Legal services ~15.3% of that revenue mix (vendor report).
- Industry synthesis citing Gartner Market Guide for Privacy Management Tools (2025) and IDC privacy-tech forecast: privacy tech on the order of **USD 12.4 billion in 2026**. Directional only.
- Thomson Reuters Institute, ALSP 2025: alternative legal services providers ~**USD 28.5 billion**. Legal-ops context, not Avelis revenue.

## Buyer context

- International Ombuds Association, 2024 Practice Survey (top findings): mixed full-time / part-time configurations; 89% employed by the organization.
- Lipsky, Seeber, et al., Fortune 1000 ADR research (Cornell): historical high mediation use among large corporates; ombuds prevalence increased in the study window. Used as qualitative buyer context, not a 2026 TAM input.
