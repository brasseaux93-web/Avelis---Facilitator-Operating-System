# Principled negotiation — case studies Avelis actually uses

These are teaching cases for the **facilitator and the process copilot**. Avelis does not lecture the live room with history. The room gets a named move, not Camp David.

Each case proves one method. Map the method, not the geopolitics.

## 1. Camp David, 1978 — interests, not positions

**Positions.** Egypt: every inch of the Sinai returned. Israel: keep at least some of the Sinai. Maps that split the peninsula satisfied neither.

**Interests.** Egypt: sovereignty (the flag). Israel: security (no tanks on the border). Compatible.

**Outcome.** Full Egyptian sovereignty; large areas demilitarized. The flag everywhere; Egyptian tanks nowhere near Israel.

**Carter’s process.** Shuttle between Sadat and Begin. They were not in the same room for most of the thirteen days. One-text drafts, not dueling maps.

**Avelis move.** `open_interests` / `ask_why` / `interest_map`. If plenary is heat: `caucus_shuttle`. Do not draw a split-the-difference map (`objective_criteria` is not “half”).

Vance later credited a process idea Fisher had given him weeks earlier. The method is the lesson, not the treaty.

## 2. Camp David one-text — single text

The Harvard Negotiation Project’s one-text procedure: a third party holds *one* draft. Parties criticize it; the third revises; no one is asked to accept until the end.

Used at Camp David; used again as the skeleton of the Algeria-mediated one-text in the **Iran hostage** endgame (1979–81), after Fisher’s team had been asked by both sides for analysis.

**Avelis move.** `single_text`. The joint minute is the one text. Written only from items already marked agreed. The facilitator drafts; parties correct labels. Avelis does not author the minute.

## 3. Law of the Sea — independent standard

Sebenius’s account of UNCLOS: years of positional haggling over seabed mining. Movement came when parties looked for **criteria neither side owned** (formulas, ratios, published rules) instead of a battle of wills over who would “win” the resource.

**Avelis move.** `objective_criteria`. Ask: what policy, precedent, or published rule would make this fair? **Avelis does not pick the number.** If someone says “split it fifty-fifty, that’s fair,” that is still a position dressed as legitimacy.

## 4. Follett’s library window — invent options before evaluating

Two people in a library. One wants the window open; one wants it shut. Positions collide. Interests: air vs. not a draft on the papers. Option: open a window in the next room.

**Avelis move.** `expand_options` only *after* `ask_why`. A demand is a closed window. Ask what it protects, then one option each that is not the opening position.

## 5. Caney Creek mine, ~1978 — process, not merits (Ury / Brett / Goldberg)

Bituminous coal, thousands of wildcat strikes a year. The intervention did **not** mediate each grievance on the merits. Ury sat with process: meetings happen, interests get said, rights-talk waits. Formal grievances collapsed; productivity rose; the system was still in use eight years later.

**Avelis move.** The whole product: facilitator writes process; agent does not decide who is right. `park_blocker` when one item is eating the session. Dispute-systems design is the buyer’s work; Avelis is the room that session runs in.

## 6. Kaiser Permanente, 2000 — interest-based bargaining at scale

KP and a coalition of 26 local unions. Historic IBN contract. Working groups **stopped exchanging proposals first**. Sequence: define the problem → interests → options → criteria → select. Fisher, in a later exchange: people invent more freely when they know **they are not yet committing**.

**Avelis move.** `ground_rules` (not yet committing) → `frame_label` → `interest_map` → `expand_options` → `objective_criteria` → `yesable` / `single_text`. Do not let a party table a package as the first act.

## What these cases do **not** license

| Temptation | Why it is out |
|---|---|
| Avelis scores BATNA / trial EV | Advocacy. `reality_test_process` only asks what dies when *this room* closes. |
| Avelis names “the fair split” | That is a position. Independent standard is a *question*. |
| Lecture the parties with Sinai or Kaiser | Performing expertise. The room gets a two-to-four-sentence move. |
| Treat shuttle as secrecy from the process record | Caucus talk is not stored. Process facts can return. Quotes cannot. |
| Imply Camp David “proves” Avelis | Different object, different stakes, different third. |

## How the copilot should use this

If a facilitator asks “what would principled negotiation do here?”, answer with **the next named move** and, at most, one analog (“this is a sovereignty/security split, not a border”). Never a history lesson in the live room.
